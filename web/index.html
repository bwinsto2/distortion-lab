Participant controls in the stereoscope

Eye:

When on, the participant works entirely inside the stereoscope with the mouse, the knobs and buttons, or the keyboard. Three steps: Choose (8 starting pictures in a ring around the centre; with both eyes, the amblyopic eye's test grating appears in the middle), Adjust (one control at a time) and Compare (flip through nearby versions). Keyboard: ← → turn knob (Shift = ×5) · ↑ ↓ choose control / page · Enter = press knob · Space = switch eye (amblyopic → fellow → both) · M = mode (Choose → Adjust → Compare) · N = new alternatives · Z = undo. Keys work in this window (outside text boxes) and in the stimulus window.
