Model, calibration & dial integration

This prototype implements the image-synthesis structure of Olianezhad et al. (2025), Eqs. 1–2: Fᵢ = clip(Gᵢ + Kᵢ, −Tᵢ, Tᵢ), and P = clip(Σ WᵢFᵢ, −1, 1). Gᵢ is a unit sinusoidal carrier. The final clip is explicitly fixed at ±1 in this implementation. K controls light/dark balance; T clips each component. A common aperture and output contrast are applied after synthesis.

The GUI directly adjusts Wᵢ. It does not implement Eq. 3’s stimulus-convolution weight calculation, the authors’ optimizer, their neural cortical-spread simulation, or a validated replication of their code. Gaussian windowing is an optional extension; the paper used circular grating stimuli. The opening identity matches the reference exactly. The form examples are independent constructions; their parameters were not copied from the authors. This version allows up to 12 components and weights up to 20 for sharp constructions; these are interface ranges, not physiological bounds.

Display values are encoded grayscale, not calibrated luminance. No gamma correction, optical calibration, timing certification, or participant protocol is supplied. Configure measured CSS pixels per visual degree at a fixed browser zoom, viewing distance, and optical path. Exported records include display geometry, device pixel ratio, settings, and stimulus snapshots. Resize/zoom changes geometry.
