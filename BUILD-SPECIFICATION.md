Scientific purpose and context:
The researcher tests anisometropic, strabismic and mixed amblyopes with a dichoptic mirror stereoscope. Participants reconstruct perceived distortions of a grating viewed through the amblyopic eye, using an adjustable reconstruction viewed through the fellow eye. Later the researcher may compare sober and psilocybin sessions in the same participants. Build a percept-matching research prototype, not medical diagnosis or a drug-administration protocol.

The existing prototype was a standalone HTML GUI implementing a mixture of rectified gratings. Its ten saved Barrett-inspired forms are included at the end of this prompt. They are INDEPENDENT ILLUSTRATIVE constructions, not authors' fitted parameters, clinical scotomas, participant percepts, or validated replicas. Preserve this provenance and the stated limitations (especially abrupt offset and three-branch gap). Source: Olianezhad et al. 2025, DOI 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.1111-25.2025, PMC12590119. A separate Maruya et al. preprint PMC12262670 used preset selection followed by dichoptic plaid matching; do not conflate those studies.

Original synthesis math, for reference and backward compatibility:
G_i(x,y)=cos(2*pi*f_i*(-x*sin(theta_i)+y*cos(theta_i))+phase_i).
Angles and phase stored in degrees, coordinates in nominal degrees, y positive upward.
F_i=clip(G_i+K_i,-T_i,T_i).
P=clip(sum_i W_i*F_i,-1,1).
pixel=round(127.5*(1+contrast*common_aperture(x,y)*P)).
K is JSON field duty, T=threshold, W=weight. W is direct and NOT normalized. The common aperture is 0 outside diameter/2; inside it is either 1 (circle) or exp(-(x*x+y*y)/(2*sigma*sigma)).
This implements the image-synthesis family of Eqs 1-2 with our explicit final clip +/-1. It does NOT implement Eq3 stimulus-derived weights, the authors' optimizer or neural cortical-spread model. Old UI ranges: max12 components, weight0..20, angle0..180 step.1, f.1..24 step.01, phase0..360 step1, K-1..1 step.01,T0..2 step.01. These are UI bounds, not physiological bounds. Gaussian windowing is an extension.
Keep units, coordinate convention, parameters and pixel output consistent across frontend/backend or document and test any differences. No gamma or timing validation is supplied merely by using this prototype.
